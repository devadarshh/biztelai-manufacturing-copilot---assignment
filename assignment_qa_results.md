## 3. Who is allowed to remove a lockout lock from a machine?

**Result:** answered

Generally, each authorised person removes only their own lock; nobody may remove another person's lock [SAF-SOP-003_Lockout_Tagout_Procedure.pdf, Page 1].

If an authorised person has left the plant without removing their lock, the lock may be removed through a specific procedure:
1.  The Shift Supervisor makes every reasonable effort to contact the lock owner by phone [SAF-SOP-003_Lockout_Tagout_Procedure.pdf, Page 1].
2.  If the owner cannot be reached, the Production Manager and the Safety Officer must both authorise removal [SAF-SOP-003_Lockout_Tagout_Procedure.pdf, Page 1].

## 9. What is the procedure for removing a lock when its owner is absent?

**Result:** answered

The procedure for removing a lock when its owner is absent is as follows:
1.  The Shift Supervisor makes every reasonable effort to contact the lock owner by phone [SAF-SOP-003_Lockout_Tagout_Procedure.pdf, Page 1].
2.  If the owner cannot be reached, the Production Manager and the Safety Officer must both authorise removal [SAF-SOP-003_Lockout_Tagout_Procedure.pdf, Page 1].
3.  The machine is physically inspected to confirm it is safe to re-energise [SAF-SOP-003_Lockout_Tagout_Procedure.pdf, Page 1].
4.  The removal is recorded on form SF-12 Lock Removal Record [SAF-SOP-003_Lockout_Tagout_Procedure.pdf, Page 2].
5.  The lock owner must be informed before they resume work on their next shift [SAF-SOP-003_Lockout_Tagout_Procedure.pdf, Page 2].
